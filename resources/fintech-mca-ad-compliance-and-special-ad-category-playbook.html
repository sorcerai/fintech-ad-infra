Fintech & MCA Ad Compliance: Platform Review and Campaign Readiness

Focus: Platform Review and Campaign Readiness • Reading Time: 14 Min • Updated: Q3 2026

Commercial lending, Merchant Cash Advance (MCA), and debt consolidation require careful campaign structure, clear disclosures, and attention to the platform rules that govern financial-services advertising.

1. Surviving Meta Special Ad Category (Credit / HEC) Restrictions

Meta strictly enforces Special Ad Category declarations for all business financing, credit cards, and personal loans. Under these rules:

  • Lookalike audiences are permanently disabled.
  • Age targeting is locked to 18–65+. Gender targeting cannot be filtered.
  • Geographic radius cannot be narrower than a 15-mile circle around a designated city.
  • Campaign and Creative Review: Because financial-services campaigns can face targeting limits, creative, disclosures, and conversion paths should be reviewed against the applicable platform requirements.

2. Regulatory Scrutiny: CFPB, FTC & Google Financial Services Verification

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) and FTC closely inspect commercial lending funnels. Ad platforms automatically flag and terminate ad accounts that exhibit:

  • Unsubstantiated loan approval guarantees or false "government funding" claims.
  • Failure to present clear representative APR examples or factor rate disclosures on the first screen of the landing page.
  • Aggressive debt relief promises claiming total eradication of principal balances without fee disclosures.
